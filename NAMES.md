# Naming — how we got to "winnow"

The project began as **meterfinder** and was renamed during the v2 rewrite. The
name had to be **commodity-neutral** (ERT covers electric, gas, and water), so
electricity puns were ruled out. We landed on **winnow** — to winnow is to
separate the wheat from the chaff, which is exactly the job: isolate the one
meter that's yours from dozens of anonymous broadcasts.

## Candidates considered

### Greek myth — watcher / messenger / metrology
- **Argus** — hundred-eyed giant; watches every meter at once
- **Hermes** — messenger god; relays readings to HA
- **Heron** — Hero of Alexandria, engineer of measuring devices
- **Panoptes** — "all-seeing" (Argus's epithet)
- **Lynceus** — Argonaut who saw through walls / at distance
- **Tiresias** — blind seer of hidden truths

### Detective / dowsing / watcher
- **Wattson** — watt + Watson; deduces which meter is yours *(electric-only ✗)*
- **Dowser** — finds what's hidden (water-witching → your meter)
- **Strix** — Greek night-owl; silent watcher of the airwaves
- **Sentinel** — watchful always-on guardian of 900 MHz

### Augury / method / RF / brandable
- **Augur** — reads the signs to reveal which meter is yours
- **Fingerprint** — identify by load fingerprint (the actual method)
- **Squelch** — radio/SDR term; playful, signals-flavored
- **Telltale** — gauge that reveals a hidden state / reveals a secret
- **Cipher** — deciphers your signal among many
- **Lodestar** — a guiding star to the right meter

### Electricity-themed — RULED OUT (not commodity-neutral)
- **Volta**, **Voltaire**, **Wattson**, **Wattcher** (any watt/volt/amp pun)

### Fresh, commodity-neutral
- **Aether** — the medium signals travel through; passively listening to the air
- **Winnow** ✅ — separate your meter from the chaff of dozens *(chosen)*
- **Census** — enumerate every meter, then find yours
- **Tracer** — trace your controlled load to the meter that responds
- **Sift**, **Quaesitor**, **Vigil**, **Rosetta**
